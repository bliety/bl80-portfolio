What to watch for

The editor's craft

Structure and suspense

How a piece sets up a question, reveals information, and lands its emotional or informational payoff.

Rhythm and restraint

Cut timing, pacing, meaningful pauses, montage construction and transitions that support the story rather than distract.

Sound and visual continuity

Audio cues, music placement, scene continuity, color and graphics used to create a coherent experience.

Communication that lands

Editing around a clear purpose so that the audience knows what matters and why.
