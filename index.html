What to watch for

The editor's craft

Structure and suspense

How a piece sets up a question, reveals information, and lands its emotional or informational payoff.

Rhythm and restraint

Cut timing, pacing, meaningful pauses, montage construction and transitions that support the story rather than distract.

Sound and visual continuity

Audio cues, music placement, scene continuity, color and graphics used to create a coherent experience.

Communication that lands

Editing around a clear purpose so that the audience knows what matters and why.

04 / My approach

Good stories are designed to reach people.

Discover

Understand the audience

Start with the audience, the context and the outcome. Identify the message and choose the channel that serves it best.

Create

Produce with purpose

Develop an editorial plan, create platform-appropriate content and bring the story to life through words, visuals, video and live coverage.

Deliver

Make it usable

Publish with care, check for clarity and accuracy, and use audience feedback and performance data to improve the next story.
